What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

A KakoBuy spreadsheet is a product directory shoppers use to shortlist marketplace listings before ordering through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses HipoBuyFind as its catalog source. Its product pages list buying options, including KakoBuy where offered.

How to check a listing before ordering

  1. Match the listing. Open the HipoBuyFind item, check the source seller link and record the marketplace item ID, colour and size. If you are comparing two offers, compare the same variant.
  2. Check measurements. For shoes, ask whether the chart lists foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare flat garment measurements with a similar piece you own.
  3. Review warehouse QC photos. If your agent supplies quality-control photos, compare the labels, colour and measurements with your order. Ask about return deadlines if anything differs.
  4. Compare parcel quotes. Use your destination and estimated packed dimensions. Check whether each shipping line bills by actual or volumetric weight and compare any applicable fees.

Estimate the full order cost

Add the item price, delivery to the warehouse, international shipping and other charges. Convert every amount to the same currency first. Include applicable taxes, duties and service fees in other charges.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Both main buttons open the HipoBuyFind homepage. Each product name opens its individual HipoBuyFind listing, where you can check the source and available buying options.

Do the product photos show my actual order?

They show catalog listings. Check any warehouse QC photos tied to your specific order before approving the parcel.

What if the source listing is sold out?

Return to the matching category and find another listing. Compare the new seller, size chart and variant price before adding it to your order.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The catalog shows the item price. Use a separate shipping quote based on your destination and parcel size to estimate your total.
